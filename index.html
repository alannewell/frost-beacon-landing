Section 03 · Executive Brief

Strategic Readout

Case Study Example

The following case study provides an illustrative example of the executive insights, strategic recommendations, and action plan delivered as part of the BEACON AI Brand Equity & Market Presence Assessment.

Business Challenge

A NASDAQ-listed deep-tech provider entering a new market, was being classified by AI answer engines according to its legacy business. To gain customers in the new market, the company needed to change this.

Key Findings

01

AI engines associated the client with a legacy market position.

02

Competitors controlled disproportionate AI visibility.

03

Buyer questions focused on unwanted topics.

04

Target market remained undefined in AI engines.

Strategic Conclusions

Visibility & Positioning

The challenge was visibility and positioning, not reputation repair. The client could own a unique market narrative before competitors.

Authoritative Sources

AI answer engines favored authoritative third-party sources.

Executive Action Plan

Five prioritized recommendations to strengthen AI market presence and establish category leadership.

1Build authoritative content aligned to buyer questions.
2Expand AI-citable source footprint across third-party media.
3Increase category association in AI-generated answers.
4Close gaps in analyst and industry publication coverage.
5Govern AI perception continuously through BEACON monitoring.

By implementing our recommended 7-point action plan, the client strengthened its AI-defined market position, increased its visibility and sentiment, and differentiated itself from its peers to establish clear ownership in its category narrative.
